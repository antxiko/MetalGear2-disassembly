What is inside

The game

  • The maps: the 20 zones, room by room, with their doors, special rooms and enemies.
  • Snake: his 98 drawings, the weapons (F2), the equipment (F3) and the radar.
  • The enemies: the 17 kinds of actor with all their drawings.
  • The bosses: the eleven fights, played by the cartridge.
  • The transceiver: the frequencies, who answers, the portraits and every conversation.
  • The texts: the font, its 128 kanji, the signs and the rest of the messages.
  • The music: the 44 tunes, from its own sound engine, and where each one is asked for.
  • The sequences: the intro, the title and the ending, at the MSX's own pace.

Curiosities

  • Curiosities: VRAM:OIK, the 140.07 radio, Konami and the MSX inside the game, SNATCHER, the open questions.
  • The debug monitor: the programmers' tool left in bank 63, switched off by two bytes.

Under the hood

  • Under the hood: rebuilding the ROM, the mapper, the interrupt in RAM, the states, the RAM, the VRAM and the tools.
